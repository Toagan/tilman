The mistake that taught me the most

In September, four large banks turned down our outreach. I assumed the copy was wrong. Before rewriting it, I checked who had actually received the emails: a private wealth adviser, a head of media relations, someone on a regulation desk. None of them could buy or use a data licence.

I ran the same check across everything we had sent: nine job functions, 2,304 contacts, not one positive reply. It was a targeting failure, and I had been about to fix the wrong thing.

I added a gate on job function before any list goes out. A wrong-person email is worse than no email, because it burns an account you wanted to approach properly. That is why I qualify hard before anything reaches a senior seller.
